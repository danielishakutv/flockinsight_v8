**FlockInsight is an all-in-one church management platform built for modern churches and fellowships.** It brings your people, attendance, giving, communication, content and online presence into one simple, beautiful app that works on any phone, tablet or computer — with no installation required.

This document is a complete tour of what FlockInsight does and the difference it makes for a local church, a fellowship, a campus ministry or a multi-branch denomination.

## Core features

### 1. Member management
Build a complete, searchable directory of your congregation. Capture names, gender, phone, email, full address, date of birth and milestones (wedding, baptism and custom anniversaries). Add a profile photo for each member. Import your whole list from a spreadsheet in minutes, and export it any time.

**How it helps:** No more lost records or scattered contact lists. Every member's details, history and milestones are in one place — ready for attendance, follow-up, messaging and pastoral care.

### 2. Attendance tracking
Take fast headcounts in seconds — total, male, female, children, first-timers and new converts — for any service or event. Review full attendance history, edit past records, and watch trends build automatically.

**How it helps:** Know your real numbers week to week. Spot growth, dips and seasonal patterns early, and make decisions with facts instead of guesses.

### 3. Dashboard & analytics
A clean dashboard shows your key numbers at a glance, plus upcoming birthdays and anniversaries and a personal to-do list. Analytics turns your data into weekly trends, growth charts, gender/age breakdowns and service comparisons.

**How it helps:** Leadership gets a living picture of church health — attendance momentum, giving totals and engagement — without anyone building a single spreadsheet.

### 4. Groups & ministries
Organise people into ministries, departments, home cells, committees and classes. Assign one or more leaders to each group, give people titles, and see every group a member belongs to from their profile.

**How it helps:** Your church structure is reflected in the system, making it easy to communicate with a choir, a department or a cell, and to see who serves where.

### 5. Giving & finance
Record tithes, offerings, donations and project gifts under your own categories. Track amount, date, giver (member or visitor) and method. See monthly, yearly and all-time totals plus per-category breakdowns, and export everything for your records.

**How it helps:** Transparent, organised financial records — perfect for accountability, board reports and year-end summaries — in your local currency.

### 6. Follow-up & visitor care
Visitors and new converts flow automatically into a follow-up pipeline: New → Contacted → In progress → Joined. Log every visit, call, WhatsApp, email or note, assign people to team members, and send an SMS straight from a contact's profile.

**How it helps:** No first-timer falls through the cracks. Your care team has a shared, accountable system that turns visitors into committed members.

### 7. Communication — SMS, email & staff notices
Reach your church in bulk, by group, or one-to-one. Send branded SMS (from your own approved sender ID), free emails, or internal staff notices. Personalise messages with placeholders like the member's name and church name, and keep a full log of what was sent.

**How it helps:** Announcements, encouragement and urgent updates reach the right people instantly — through the channels Africans actually use.

### 8. Devotionals & newsletters
Write devotionals and newsletters with a cover image, then send them by email to your members, your subscribers, or both. Send immediately or **schedule** them for a specific date and time — ideal for a daily devotional or a weekly newsletter. A newsletter sign-up form is built into your public church page, so **anyone** (not just members) can subscribe. Manage subscribers with a live count and one-click CSV export.

**How it helps:** Keep your congregation — and the wider community — spiritually fed and informed all week, automatically. Grow a mailing list beyond your membership and nurture it with consistent, scheduled content.

### 9. Forms (a Google-Forms-style builder)
Create your own forms in minutes — registrations, surveys, prayer requests, volunteer sign-ups and more. Choose from many field types (short text, paragraph, email, phone, number, date, dropdown, multiple choice, checkboxes, yes/no), mark fields required, and reorder them. Pick your own shareable link, then collect responses with a **live count**, CSV export, and the option to **automatically create a member** from each submission. Get an email and in-app alert on every response.

**How it helps:** Stop paying for separate form tools or sending people to third-party sites. Collect data on your own branded link, keep it inside your church account, and turn sign-ups directly into members and follow-up.

### 10. Media library & storage
Upload sermons, photos, documents and any other files. Images and videos are **automatically optimised and resized** to save space. Every file gets a shareable link, a download option and inline audio/video players. Each church starts with 200MB of free storage and a live usage bar; upgrade with affordable monthly add-ons when you need more.

**How it helps:** A central home for sermon recordings, bulletins, flyers and photos — easy to share with members and light on storage.

### 11. Events & programs
Publish events and programs with flyers, dates, times and venues. Public events appear on your church's public page and in the public events directory automatically.

**How it helps:** Promote crusades, conferences and special services to your members and to people discovering your church online.

### 12. Your public church page
Every church gets a **modern, landing-page-style public site** at a link you choose (e.g. `flockinsight.com/c/grace-chapel`). It showcases your logo, cover image, photo gallery, service times, upcoming events, location with directions, contact details and social links — and includes a built-in newsletter sign-up. Choose from **7 colour themes** to match your church's identity. Your church can also appear in the public church **directory** so seekers can find you.

**How it helps:** A professional online presence with zero web-design work — a page members proudly share to invite friends, and a front door for newcomers searching for a church near them.

### 13. Automatic reminders & celebrations
Send members **automatic service reminders** by email and/or SMS — the day before or on the day, at a time you choose, with templates you control. Send **automatic birthday and anniversary messages** (weddings, baptisms and custom milestones) so no one is ever forgotten.

**How it helps:** Higher attendance and a congregation that feels personally cared for — all on autopilot, in your church's voice.

### 14. Notifications & web push
In-app notifications keep your team updated on important events (new form responses, follow-up assignments, account changes). Optional web push delivers alerts to phones even when the app is closed. A live notification bell shows unread updates at a glance.

**How it helps:** Your team stays in the loop and responds quickly, without checking email all day.

### 15. Roles, permissions & team
Invite your team by email and control exactly what each person can see and do with custom roles (e.g. Usher, Finance, Pastor). The church owner always has full access; everyone else gets precisely the access you grant.

**How it helps:** Delegate confidently. Finance sees giving, ushers record attendance, pastors see everything — with sensitive data protected.

### 16. Wallet, plans & billing
A single church **wallet** funds SMS and storage upgrades — top up once via card or transfer. Choose a subscription **plan** that fits your size (with a free Starter tier), upgrade as you grow, and view your full payment history. Payments are handled securely through Paystack.

**How it helps:** Predictable, pay-as-you-grow pricing with no surprises, and one simple balance for all your usage-based features.

### 17. Mobile app experience (PWA)
FlockInsight installs on phones and computers like a native app, works **offline**, and has a thumb-friendly mobile layout with a fast "Record attendance" button always within reach.

**How it helps:** Ushers and leaders use it comfortably on their phones in the service — even with patchy network.

### 18. Reports & exports
Export attendance to CSV or a branded PDF report, export members, giving, form responses and subscribers to CSV — all ready for Excel, Google Sheets, board meetings or audits.

**How it helps:** Your data is never locked in. Produce clean reports for leadership and records in one click.

### 19. Help & support
Built-in, step-by-step guides for every feature, plus a direct support ticket system that reaches a real human by email.

**How it helps:** Your team is never stuck — answers are inside the app, and help is one message away.

### 20. Security, reliability & data ownership
Each church's data is isolated and secured, with role-based access, rate-limited logins and **daily encrypted backups** (kept on-site and off-site). The platform runs on its own infrastructure behind Cloudflare with SSL.

**How it helps:** Member information is protected and recoverable — peace of mind for pastors and members alike.
