# FlockInsight — feature roadmap & "must-haves"

Built so far (this round): public church pages + directory, events module, bulk
SMS/email + sender-ID (Termii), automatic service reminders, birthday/anniversary
auto-messages, support tickets + help guides, platform analytics, superadmin user
management + password reset, featured churches + ad banners.

Below: the high-impact features churches/fellowships repeatedly ask for. Ordered
roughly by "makes it impossible to say no."

## Tier 1 — biggest pull (build next)

1. **Online giving / donations.** Let members give online (Paystack), set up
   recurring giving, and instantly record it. This is the single most-requested
   feature and a revenue driver (platform fee). Pairs with #2.
2. **Giving statements & receipts.** Auto-generate per-member contribution
   statements (monthly/yearly) as PDF — essential for serious givers & audits.
3. **Children's check-in & security.** Secure child check-in with guardian
   matching and matching pickup labels/codes. Safety sells; parents love it.
4. **Visitor / first-timer capture form.** A public QR/web form a visitor fills
   on arrival → drops straight into Follow-up. Closes the "they visit once and
   vanish" gap.
5. **Member self-service.** A light member login (or magic link) to update their
   own details, see giving history, RSVP to events, and get notifications.
   (PWA already exists — this extends it to the congregation.)
6. **WhatsApp broadcasts.** Huge in Africa. Termii already supports a WhatsApp
   channel — extend Communication + reminders to send via WhatsApp.

## Tier 2 — strong differentiators

7. **Volunteer scheduling / rosters.** Schedule ushers, choir, media, ushering
   teams with reminders and swap requests.
8. **Prayer requests.** Members submit prayer requests (public form or in-app);
   pastoral team manages and assigns.
9. **Sermon / media library.** Upload or link audio/video sermons, notes and
   series; show on the public church page.
10. **Forms & surveys builder.** Custom registration/feedback forms with
    responses (event registration, membership class sign-ups).
11. **Discipleship tracking.** Track membership/baptism classes, courses and
    spiritual-growth milestones per member.
12. **Smart segments & "we missed you".** Auto-detect members who stopped
    attending (e.g. no attendance 30 days) and trigger follow-up; dynamic groups
    (new-this-month, birthdays-this-week) for targeted comms.

## Tier 3 — depth & scale

13. **Two-way SMS / inbox.** Receive replies and hold conversations.
14. **Pledges & campaigns.** Building-fund pledges with progress thermometers.
15. **Church calendar + room/resource booking.**
16. **Multi-branch / campus management** for denominations (enterprise tier).
17. **Finance controls + audit log.** Approvals, who-changed-what trail.
18. **Offline-first attendance.** Record offline, sync later (low connectivity).
19. **Localization.** Yoruba / Hausa / Igbo / French for broader reach.
20. **Onboarding wizard + sample data** to cut time-to-value, and a
    church-to-church **referral program** for a growth loop.

## Quick wins / polish

- Member tags; per-ministry dashboards; data self-export; richer email
  (newsletter) builder; public church reviews (moderated); cover-image cards in
  the directory; “add to calendar” on event pages; QR code generator for the
  public page & events.
