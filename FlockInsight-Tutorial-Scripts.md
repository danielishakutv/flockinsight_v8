# FlockInsight — Video Tutorial Scripts

A complete, lesson-by-lesson script for recording FlockInsight tutorial videos. Each **lesson** is a short, self-contained "byte" (30 seconds to 3 minutes) with a goal, the on-screen path, numbered steps, and a suggested narration. Record them in order for a full course, or individually as help clips.

**Legend**
- **Path:** where to click (menu → page).
- **Do:** the exact on-screen steps.
- **Say:** suggested voice-over.
- **Tip:** an on-screen callout.

> Menus: on desktop the left sidebar; on mobile the bottom bar + **More** sheet. "Record attendance" is the big + button, always visible.

---

## Module 0 — Welcome & setup

### Lesson 0.1 — What is FlockInsight? (60s)
- **Say:** "FlockInsight is your church in one app — attendance, members, giving, follow-up, messaging, devotionals, forms and your own public church page. It works on any phone or computer, even offline. Let's get you set up in a few minutes."
- **Do:** Pan across the dashboard, then the left menu.

### Lesson 0.2 — Create your church account (90s)
- **Path:** flockinsight.com → **Get started / Sign up**.
- **Do:** 1) Enter your name, church name, email and password. 2) Submit. 3) Verify your email if prompted.
- **Say:** "Signing up creates your church automatically. You're the owner, with full access."
- **Tip:** One account = one church. You can invite your team later.

### Lesson 0.3 — Church basics (timezone, currency, country) (90s)
- **Path:** **Settings → General**.
- **Do:** 1) Set church name, country and state. 2) Set timezone and currency. 3) Save.
- **Say:** "Timezone controls when reminders send; currency shows on all your giving. Set these once."

### Lesson 0.4 — A tour of the menu (60s)
- **Say:** "Down the left you'll find Dashboard, Attendance, Analytics, Members, Groups, Giving, Follow-up, Media, Forms, Devotionals and Settings. On mobile they're in the bottom bar and the More sheet."

## Module 1 — Members

### Lesson 1.1 — Add your first member (90s)
- **Path:** **Members → Add member**.
- **Do:** 1) Add a profile photo (optional). 2) Enter name, gender, phone and email. 3) Add date of birth and address. 4) Save.
- **Say:** "Phone and email let you reach members by SMS and email later. Date of birth powers automatic birthday wishes."

### Lesson 1.2 — Import members from a spreadsheet (2m)
- **Path:** **Members → Import**.
- **Do:** 1) Download the template. 2) Fill your list. 3) Upload the file. 4) Review the per-row report.
- **Say:** "Already have a list? Import everyone at once. We match columns by their headers and tell you if any rows were skipped."
- **Tip:** Export any time from **Members → Export**.

### Lesson 1.3 — The member profile (60s)
- **Path:** **Members →** click a member.
- **Do:** Show the read-only profile, groups they belong to, and milestones. Click **Edit** to change details.
- **Say:** "Everything about a person lives here — contact details, milestones and the groups they serve in."

### Lesson 1.4 — Milestones & anniversaries (60s)
- **Do:** On a member, add wedding date, baptism, and any custom anniversary.
- **Say:** "Add milestones and FlockInsight will celebrate them automatically — and remind you on the dashboard."

## Module 2 — Attendance

### Lesson 2.1 — Set up your services (60s)
- **Path:** **Settings → Services → Add**.
- **Do:** Name the service, set the day and start time. Save.
- **Say:** "Your services power attendance recording, your public page and automatic reminders."

### Lesson 2.2 — Record attendance in seconds (90s)
- **Path:** The **+ Record** button.
- **Do:** 1) Pick the service and date. 2) Tap the steppers for total, male, female, children and first-timers. 3) Save.
- **Say:** "Fast headcounts — no logins for members, no fuss. Your dashboard updates instantly."

### Lesson 2.3 — History, edit & export (90s)
- **Path:** **Attendance**.
- **Do:** Open a past session to edit it; use the export options for CSV or a branded PDF report.
- **Say:** "Review any past service, fix a number, or download a clean PDF report for your board."

## Module 5 — Giving

### Lesson 5.1 — Set up giving categories (60s)
- **Path:** **Settings → Giving**.
- **Do:** Add categories like Tithe, Offering, Building.
- **Say:** "Define the categories your church uses once."

### Lesson 5.2 — Record a gift (90s)
- **Path:** **Giving → Add**.
- **Do:** Choose category, amount, date, and optionally the giver and method. Save.
- **Say:** "Record tithes and offerings in your currency. Totals and breakdowns update on the dashboard and analytics."

### Lesson 5.3 — Reports & export (45s)
- **Say:** "See monthly, yearly and all-time totals, and export to CSV for your records at any time."

## Module 6 — Follow-up & visitor care

### Lesson 6.1 — The follow-up pipeline (90s)
- **Path:** **Follow-up**.
- **Say:** "Visitors and new converts arrive here automatically. Move each person through stages: New, Contacted, In progress, Joined."

### Lesson 6.2 — Log interactions & assign (90s)
- **Do:** Open a person → log a visit, call or note; assign to a team member; send an SMS from their profile.

## Module 7 — Communication

### Lesson 7.1 — Send SMS, email or a notice (2m)
- **Path:** **Communication**.
- **Do:** 1) Choose a channel. 2) Pick recipients (everyone, a group, or selected members). 3) Use a template with {name} and {church}. 4) Send.
- **Say:** "Reach your whole church, a single group, or one person — personalised, and logged."

### Lesson 7.2 — Get your SMS sender ID (2m)
- **Path:** **Settings → SMS**.
- **Do:** Enter the name recipients will see (3–11 characters). Submit for approval; check status.
- **Say:** "Your SMS come from your church's name. We submit it for approval — usually a few hours."

### Lesson 7.3 — Fund your wallet (60s)
- **Path:** **Settings → Wallet**.
- **Do:** Top up via card or transfer.
- **Say:** "One wallet funds your SMS and storage upgrades. Top up once."

## Module 8 — Automatic messages

### Lesson 8.1 — Service reminders (2m)
- **Path:** **Settings → Reminders**.
- **Do:** Turn on, pick channels, day-before or same-day, time, audience, and edit templates. Send yourself a test.
- **Say:** "Automatically remind members before every service — by email or SMS — in your church's own words."

### Lesson 8.2 — Birthday & anniversary messages (90s)
- **Path:** **Settings → Celebrations**.
- **Do:** Turn on, choose channels and send time, edit the templates.
- **Say:** "Never miss a birthday again — FlockInsight wishes members automatically on their special days."

## Module 9 — Media library

### Lesson 9.1 — Upload sermons, photos & files (2m)
- **Path:** **Media → Upload files**.
- **Do:** 1) Pick a category (Sermon, Photo, Document). 2) Choose files. 3) Watch the background progress bar.
- **Say:** "Upload anything — audio, video, images, PDFs. Images and video are automatically shrunk to save space. Uploads keep running even if you switch pages."

### Lesson 9.2 — Preview, share & download (90s)
- **Do:** Click a file to preview (image lightbox, or audio/video player). Use Copy link or Download.
- **Say:** "Play sermons right here, copy a shareable link, or download the original."

### Lesson 9.3 — Storage & upgrades (60s)
- **Path:** **Settings → Storage**.
- **Say:** "Every church gets 200MB free. Need more? Add a monthly storage bundle, paid from your wallet."

## Module 10 — Forms

### Lesson 10.1 — Build a form (2m)
- **Path:** **Forms → New form**.
- **Do:** 1) Add a title. 2) Add questions and pick answer types. 3) Mark required, reorder with the arrows. 4) Choose your link name.
- **Say:** "Build registrations, surveys and sign-ups in minutes — with your own link, like /f/easter-2026."

### Lesson 10.2 — Publish & share (45s)
- **Do:** Set to **Live**, copy the link, share it.
- **Say:** "Anyone can fill it in — no account needed. Set it to Closed to stop collecting."

### Lesson 10.3 — Responses, live (90s)
- **Path:** **Forms →** open a form → **Responses**.
- **Say:** "Responses appear live as they arrive. Export to CSV, and — if you like — each submission can automatically become a member and enter follow-up."

## Module 11 — Devotionals & newsletters

### Lesson 11.1 — Write a devotional or newsletter (2m)
- **Path:** **Devotionals → New devotional / New newsletter**.
- **Do:** Add a title, optional cover image, and your message. Choose the audience: members, subscribers, or both.
- **Say:** "Feed your church all week. Write once, choose who receives it."

### Lesson 11.2 — Send now or schedule (60s)
- **Do:** Send immediately, or schedule for a date and time.
- **Say:** "Perfect for a daily devotional or a weekly newsletter — set it and forget it."

### Lesson 11.3 — Grow & manage subscribers (90s)
- **Path:** **Devotionals → Subscribers**.
- **Say:** "Anyone can subscribe from your public page — even non-members. See the live count, add people manually, and export the whole list to CSV. New subscribers get a welcome email automatically, and you're notified in the app."

## Module 19 — For platform administrators (superadmin)

> These lessons are for the FlockInsight operator, not individual churches.

### Lesson 19.1 — The admin overview (60s)
- **Path:** **/superadmin**.
- **Say:** "Platform-wide numbers, churches, users, pricing, support and the changelog — all in one place."

### Lesson 19.2 — Help a church (impersonate) (60s)
- **Path:** **Superadmin → Churches →** a church → **Log in as church**.
- **Say:** "Step into any church securely to help resolve an issue, then exit back to admin."

### Lesson 19.3 — Export a church backup (60s)
- **Path:** Church detail → **Data & recovery → Download**.
- **Say:** "Download a church's complete data as a single backup file, any time."

### Lesson 19.4 — Restore a backup as a new church (90s)
- **Do:** **Data & recovery → Restore as new →** choose a backup file.
- **Say:** "Recover data safely — a restore always creates a brand-new church and never overwrites anything."

### Lesson 19.5 — Reset a church safely (90s)
- **Do:** **Data & recovery → Reset →** download the backup, type the church name, confirm.
- **Say:** "A reset clears a church's data but keeps its account and team. It forces a backup and a typed confirmation first — and it's written to the audit log."
