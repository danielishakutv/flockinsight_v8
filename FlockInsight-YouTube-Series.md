# FlockInsight — YouTube Onboarding Series

A 17-episode tutorial series that takes a church from "what is this?" to fully running on FlockInsight. One feature per episode, 3–5 minutes each, in the order a new church actually needs them.

**Format for every episode**
- Screen recording + voiceover, using a demo church with realistic data.
- Open with the outcome ("By the end of this video you'll…"), not the feature name.
- Close with: *"Start free at flockinsight.com — your first 7 Sundays are on us."* + next-episode pointer.
- Cut one 30–60s vertical Short from each episode (its single wow moment).
- Click-by-click steps for every scene are in `FlockInsight-Tutorial-Scripts.md`.

---

## Act I — Get set up

### Ep 1 · Welcome to FlockInsight *(2–3 min — the trailer)*
Goal: someone who has never heard of FlockInsight decides to try it.
- Your church in one app: attendance, members, giving, follow-up, messaging, devotionals, forms, and your own public page.
- Built for pastors and admins — any church size, works on any phone or computer, even offline.
- 30-second silent sweep of the dashboard: let the product talk.
- First 7 Sundays completely free, no card required.
- What the series covers; subscribe for the rest.

### Ep 2 · Create your church & set the basics *(4–5 min)*
Goal: account created, church configured, services defined.
- Sign up at flockinsight.com — your church is created automatically and you're the owner.
- Verify your email, then Settings → General: country, state, timezone (drives reminders), currency (shows on giving).
- Settings → Services: add each weekly service — these power attendance, reminders and your public page.
- Quick menu tour: sidebar on desktop, bottom bar + More on mobile.

### Ep 3 · Add your members *(4–5 min)*
Goal: the congregation is in the system.
- Add one member: photo, name, gender, phone & email (needed for SMS/email later), birthday (powers automatic wishes).
- Have a list already? Import the whole spreadsheet with the CSV template — per-row report shows anything skipped.
- The member profile: details, groups, milestones (wedding, baptism, custom anniversaries).
- Export anytime — your data stays yours.

## Act II — Your weekly rhythm

### Ep 4 · Record attendance in seconds *(4 min)*
Goal: Sunday's headcount recorded before the benediction.
- The + Record button is always one tap away.
- Pick the service and date, then count Adults, Teens and Children — each split male / female.
- First-timers and new converts (also by gender) — counted "within" the total.
- The total adds itself; save and the dashboard updates instantly.
- Fix any past record from Attendance history; export CSV or a branded PDF for your board.

### Ep 5 · Dashboard & analytics *(4 min)*
Goal: read the numbers like a story.
- Dashboard at a glance: last service, weekly average, members, first-timers, giving this month, upcoming birthdays.
- Analytics: 12-week trend and growth %, weekly breakdown (adults / teens / children), demographics donut, average by service.
- The point: spot growth — or a dip — weeks early, and decide with facts.

### Ep 6 · Groups & ministries *(3 min)*
Goal: people organised the way the church actually works.
- Types that fit church life: ministry, department, cell, committee, class.
- Create a group, bulk-add members, mark leaders with their titles.
- Copy a group's emails or phone numbers in one click; groups appear on member profiles.
- Teaser: message a whole group at once — that's Episode 9.

### Ep 7 · Giving records *(4 min)*
Goal: clean giving records in your own currency.
- Set categories once (Settings → Giving): Tithe, Offering, Building…
- Record a gift: amount, category, date, optional giver and method.
- Month, year and all-time totals with a category breakdown.
- Export to CSV whenever the board asks.

## Act III — Care & communication

### Ep 8 · Never lose a visitor *(4 min)*
Goal: every first-timer gets followed up.
- Visitors and new converts enter the Follow-up pipeline automatically.
- Move people through stages: New → Contacted → In progress → Joined.
- Log calls, visits and notes; assign each person to a team member.
- Send an SMS right from their profile — every touch is on record.

### Ep 9 · SMS & email broadcasts *(5 min)*
Goal: reach everyone — personally.
- Choose a channel (SMS, email, staff notice) and an audience: everyone, a group, or selected people.
- Personalise with {name} and {church}; every send is logged.
- Get your SMS sender ID (Settings → SMS) so texts arrive from your church's name — approval usually takes hours.
- The wallet funds SMS: top up once by card or transfer.

### Ep 10 · Messages that send themselves *(4 min)*
Goal: reminders and celebrations on autopilot.
- Service reminders: day-before or same-day, email or SMS, in your own words — send yourself a test.
- Birthday and anniversary wishes go out automatically at your chosen time.
- Set it once; it runs every week without you.

## Act IV — Reach & grow

### Ep 11 · Sermons & media *(3 min)*
Goal: one tidy home for sermons, photos and documents.
- Upload anything — audio, video, images, PDFs; uploads run in the background with a progress bar.
- Files are auto-compressed to save space; 200MB free, bundles available from your wallet.
- Preview in-app (lightbox and players), copy a share link, or download the original.

### Ep 12 · Forms *(4 min)*
Goal: registrations and surveys with your own link.
- Build in minutes: questions, answer types, required fields, drag to reorder.
- Your own link — like /f/easter-2026 — anyone can fill it, no account needed.
- Responses appear live; export to CSV.
- The magic: submissions can automatically become members and enter follow-up.

### Ep 13 · Devotionals & newsletters *(4 min)*
Goal: feed the flock all week, not just Sundays.
- Write with a cover image; choose the audience — members, subscribers, or both.
- Send now or schedule: perfect for a daily devotional or weekly newsletter.
- Subscribers can join from your public page; welcome emails are automatic and you see the live count.
- Every email carries your church's name as the sender.

### Ep 14 · Events & your public church page *(5 min)*
Goal: a real web presence — no designer needed.
- Publish events with a flyer; public ones appear on your page and the events directory automatically.
- Build the page: link name, logo, cover, about, photos, contact and socials.
- Pick one of 7 colour themes; visitors get light/dark and a built-in newsletter sign-up.
- Share the link everywhere; toggle "List in directory" to be found.

---

## Act V — Run it well

### Ep 15 · Your team & permissions *(4 min)*
Goal: many hands, each with exactly the right access.
- Invite by email — they get a join link.
- Create roles (e.g. Finance) and tick precisely what each can view or manage.
- Ushers record attendance, Finance sees giving; the Owner always keeps full access.

### Ep 16 · Plans, billing & your free trial *(3 min)*
Goal: total clarity on what's free and what's paid.
- Every new church: first 7 Sundays completely free, no card required.
- Friendly reminders arrive before the trial ends; then pick a plan that fits your size.
- Settings → Billing: upgrade, renewal date, payment history.
- Note: the wallet (SMS & storage) is separate from the plan.

### Ep 17 · Launch day *(3 min — the finale)*
Goal: FlockInsight installed, supported, and live this Sunday.
- Install it like an app: Add to Home Screen — works offline, Record always a thumb away.
- Help & Support: step-by-step guides plus a ticket that reaches a real person.
- First-week checklist: services ✓ members ✓ first attendance ✓ team invited ✓ public page live ✓
- Share the series with another pastor; subscribe for what's next.
